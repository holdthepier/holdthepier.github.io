Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short

Hold the Pier does not collect any personal data. The game does not connect to the internet and contains no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts. No data is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (gold, skill tree, settings and the run in progress) is stored only on your device, in the game's own folder. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the game also deletes your save.

If your device backup (iCloud or a computer) is turned on, the game's folder is included in that backup. The backup is handled by Apple's services and by you; it does not reach us.

Permissions

The game does not ask for, and does not access, your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a feature that collects data is ever added (for example ads or in-app purchases), this page will be updated before that version of the game is released, describing what is collected and why.
