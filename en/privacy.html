Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short

Hold the Pier does not collect any personal data and we have no servers. The game has no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts of our own; no data is sent to us. The game only goes online for Apple's Game Center and iCloud saves, and can be played offline without them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores, achievements and player name are kept in Apple's Game Center; we keep nothing on a server. The leaderboard screen shows other players' names and profile photos fetched from Game Center. You can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings > Game Center.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress (gold, skill tree, records, quests, achievements and Daily Challenge history) is also kept in your Apple Account's iCloud, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new device with the same Apple Account. This copy lives on Apple's servers, in your account; we cannot see it. Settings and an unfinished run stay on this device only. You can stop the game from using iCloud in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (gold, skill tree, settings and the run in progress) is stored on your device, in the game's own folder. If iCloud is on, your progress is also synced to your Apple Account's iCloud (see above); settings and the run in progress stay on your device only. With iCloud off, deleting the game also deletes your save.

If your device backup (iCloud or a computer) is turned on, the game's folder is included in that backup. The backup is handled by Apple's services and by you; it does not reach us.

Permissions

The game does not ask for, and does not access, your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

For the Friends tab of the leaderboard, the game asks for access to your Game Center friends list; if you decline, only the Everyone list is shown.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a feature that collects data is ever added (for example ads or in-app purchases), this page will be updated before that version of the game is released, describing what is collected and why.
