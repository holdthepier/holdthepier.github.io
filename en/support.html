Where is my save?

Your save is kept on your device, in the game's own folder. Deleting the game also deletes the save. A device backup (iCloud or a computer) includes the game's folder, so restoring a device from a backup brings the save back. Saves are not synced between devices.

Which languages does the game support?

The game is in ten languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Russian, Japanese and Korean. On first launch it uses your device's language; you can change it in the game under Settings > Language.

There is no sound

If your phone's silent switch is on, the game makes no sound. Sound effects and ambience can be adjusted separately in the game's Settings.

I found a problem

When you write to us, please include:

  • what happened and what you were doing,
  • your device model and iOS / iPadOS version,
  • the game's version (in the game: Settings > Licences, at the top),
  • a screenshot or screen recording if you have one.
